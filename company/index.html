About this studio

A personal notebook of machines.

I keep this notebook of UV printing machines — what each one does, what it costs to run, and which brochure actually answers a buyer’s question. The machines are built in Guangzhou; the notes are mine.

Where they come from

Built by printers, for printers.

The workshop began in 2009 as a small digital printing shop in Guangzhou. The founders were printers first — they bought machines, fixed them, and eventually decided they could build something more reliable themselves.

Today the same workshop builds UV flatbed, rotary, roll-to-roll and onepass printers, develops its own ink systems, and supports customers in more than sixty countries. It still runs on the same idea: keep the mechanism simple, keep the parts serviceable, and answer the phone.

能力与品控
Capability & Quality

What happens between order and shipment.

Every machine is assembled, calibrated and print-tested in Guangzhou before packing.

叁

Independent R&D

An in-house R&D team works on printhead control, ink systems and colour management, with its own controller boards used across the range.

Precision machining

CNC machining centres produce critical components, and a solid natural-marble inspection bed is used to check guide rails, beams and platforms before assembly.

Eight-stage quality control

Production is split into eight stages with a named owner and timestamp for each, so any defect can be traced back to its source.

Final inspection and vibration test

Every unit passes 36 final checks before packing, then runs on a vibration table for 24 hours to confirm components stay seated in transit.

Lean production

Automated lines, intelligent warehousing and production scheduling, plus flexible changeover for small custom batches.

Global service and support

A service network covering 60+ countries with local spare-parts stock, remote diagnostics and on-site engineer visits.

Quality gates before dispatch

壹

Incoming inspection

Printheads, boards and motion parts are checked against serial numbers on arrival.

贰

Frame & motion calibration

Squareness, belt tension and carriage height are measured and recorded.

叁

Continuous test patterns on real substrates before the machine is cleaned and packed.
