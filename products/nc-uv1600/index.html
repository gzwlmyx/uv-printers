NC-UV1600

1.6 m Indoor & Outdoor Advertising Printer

Eight-colour 1.6 m roll printer for light boxes, banners and vehicle graphics.

  • 1600 mm width
  • 1200 dpi
  • Up to 14 m2/h
  • 8 colours
Get a Quote Download brochurePDF

NC-UV1600 is the 1.6 m wide-format roll printer in the range, aimed at advertising production: light boxes, KT boards, roll-up banners, wallpapers, vehicle stickers and floor graphics. An eight-colour channel set with light cyan, light magenta and light blacks smooths gradients, and the LED preview cover lets the operator check output without stopping.

  • 1.6 m print width covering light boxes, banners and wallpapers.
  • Eight-colour channel set smooths gradients and skin tones.
  • Up to 1200 dpi with a semi-transparent LED preview cover.
  • Integrated pressure-roller feeding for stable media transport.
  • Intelligent ink-consumption calculation simplifies cost control.
