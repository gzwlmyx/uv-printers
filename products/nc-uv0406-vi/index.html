NC-UV0406-VI

Multifunctional Small Digital Inkjet Printer

Sixth-generation 400 x 600 mm A2 workhorse for shop-floor customisation.

  • 400 x 600 mm
  • 0-180 mm
  • 1200 dpi
  • 143 W
Get a Quote Download brochurePDF

The NC-UV0406-VI is the sixth generation of this small flatbed line and the platform behind the 400 x 600 mm format. Dual Epson i1600-U1 heads, a 0-180 mm lift and a vacuum table handle rigid boards, boxes and thick blanks; the semi-enclosed frame keeps it usable in a shop rather than a workshop.

  • Dual Epson i1600-U1 heads reach 1200 dpi on a 400 x 600 mm bed.
  • 0-180 mm lift handles thick blanks, boxes and rigid boards.
  • Vacuum platform and semi-enclosed frame hold output stable.
  • Optional rotary fixture adds 360-degree printing on cups.
  • Sixth-generation platform, compact enough for retail studios.
