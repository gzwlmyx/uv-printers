NC-HIJ-A3

Digital Inkjet Carton Printing Machine

Carton printing with automatic load/unload and variable data.

  • 1000 mm width
  • 450 mm height
  • 1200 dpi
  • 30 m/min
Get a Quote Download brochurePDF

NC-HIJ-A3 is the carton printer for packaging lines: material up to 1000 mm wide and 450 mm high, variable data such as barcodes and serial numbers without plate making, and optional automatic loading and unloading. A three-step touchscreen workflow and positioning guide strips keep setup short between jobs.

  • Material up to 1000 mm wide and 450 mm high.
  • Variable data printing of barcodes and serial numbers, no plates.
  • Optional automatic loading and unloading equipment.
  • Positioning guides and suction conveyor hold alignment.
  • Up to 1200 dpi with a three-step touchscreen workflow.
