Industrial Rotary UV Printer (6 Heads)

Top of the rotary range: six heads, primer, 20 mm high drop.

  • 5-6 heads
  • 2-20 mm high drop
  • 30 s/piece

Get a Quote Request brochure

NC-CylinderElf-IV-D is the highest configuration in the fourth-generation rotary line: five Ricoh GEN6 heads as standard, six optional, plus primer alongside CMYK, light cyan and light magenta. 2-20 mm high-drop printing and automatic product measurement let it handle irregular curved surfaces that stop smaller rotary machines.

  • Five Ricoh GEN6 heads standard, sixth head optional.
  • Primer + CMYK + Lc + Lm + white + varnish in one pass.
  • 2-20 mm high-drop for complex curved and stepped parts.
  • Seamless 360-degree wrapping at up to 30 seconds per piece.
  • Laser collision protection and negative-pressure ink supply.
