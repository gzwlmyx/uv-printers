NC-UVDTF60-PLUS

60 cm UV DTF Laminating All-in-One Machine

60 cm UV DTF line: standard 3-head, high-end 4-head with foil stamping.

  • 600 mm width
  • 3-4 heads
  • 7.5 m/h
  • Foil stamping
Get a Quote Download brochurePDF

NC-UVDTF60-PLUS is the 60 cm UV DTF line, offered in two builds. The standard version runs three Epson i1600-E1 heads for white, colour and varnish on 600 mm film; the high-end version adds a fourth head and glue plus hot-stamping varnish, so laminated labels can carry metallic foil effects in the same pass.

  • 600 mm UV DTF film width for volume crystal-label output.
  • Standard 3-head build; high-end 4-head build adds foil stamping.
  • Peeling, printing, laminating and rewinding in one workflow.
  • White, colour and varnish printed together, up to 1200 dpi.
  • Silent guide rails, vacuum platform and low-ink warning.
