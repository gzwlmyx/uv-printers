NC-UVA3MAX

Desktop Multifunction UV Printer

A3 desktop UV printer for gifts, samples and short-run custom work.

  • 297 x 420 mm
  • 0-90 mm
  • 960 x 1200 dpi
  • CMYK + W + V
Get a Quote Download brochurePDF

The NC-UVA3MAX puts flatbed, roll-to-roll and cylindrical printing into one desktop unit. A single Epson i3200-U1HD head, built-in visual positioning and a 0-90 mm lift cover gift, promotional and prototype work without a large floor footprint. White, colour and varnish are laid down in one pass at up to 960 x 1200 dpi.

  • Single Epson i3200-U1HD head; white, colour and varnish in one pass.
  • Built-in visual positioning for pre-printed and irregular workpieces.
  • 0-90 mm print height on a desktop frame; A3 format.
  • Automatic ink circulation, low-ink alerts and anti-collision protection.
  • Optional roll and rotary fixtures extend flatbed work to film and cups.
