Case 01 · Foil stamping

Metallic foil, without a foil-stamping press.

Foil is transferred onto art paper, gift boxes and red envelopes in a single UV pass — white ink underneath, then gold or silver foil on top. No plate making, no separate hot-stamping machine, and the artwork can change between jobs.

工厂故事
Made in Guangzhou

A workshop in Guangzhou, not a trading desk.

R&D, machining, assembly, print testing and after-sales all sit under one roof — which is why a spare part question gets answered the same day.

陆

The machines in this notebook are built by a Guangzhou workshop that began in 2009 as a small digital printing shop. The same team still develops its own ink systems, builds its own frames, and tests every machine on real substrates before it leaves the floor.

They end up with decorators, sign makers, gift factories and packaging plants in more than sixty countries — most of the buyers arrived through another customer.
