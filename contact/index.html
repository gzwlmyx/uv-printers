FAQ

Before you call.

问
Do you ship worldwide?

Yes — air and sea freight from Guangzhou, with export documents prepared by our team. Tell us the nearest port and we will quote the full delivered cost.

Can you print a sample on our material?

Send a small piece of the substrate to our Guangzhou address. We print it on the recommended machine and send photos, or return the sample with the quotation.

What about installation and training?

Every machine ships with an English manual and a setup video call. On-site installation and training can be arranged for larger orders.

Where can I find the technical documents?

The Downloads page holds the current English brochures for most models; the cylinder and SLP20 sheets are sent on request. Certification and spare-parts documents are available on request.
